intereses actuales

En corto: me interesa cómo lo que documentamos, describimos y catalogamos decide lo que después se puede saber y recordar de las personas, y cómo construir tecnología en nuestro interés y no para ampliar brechas sociales ni el agotamiento de la naturaleza.

Mi trabajo actual se centra en la invisibilización del trabajo de cuidado, particularmente el cuidado feminizado, y en la ausencia más amplia de una política del cuidado en el internet: infraestructura, protocolos, bases de datos, plataformas y UX/UI. Los archivos coloniales digitales son mi sitio de indagación actual, y noto cómo este patrón se repite en los sistemas digitales generales. Me atraen la ética del cuidado, la fantología y la fabulación crítica como formas de mapear los futuros que han quedado fuera y las posibilidades pendientes de abrazar. Me gusta leer, escribir y construir contra-archivos, mapas interactivos e interfaces especulativas. Soy y estoy en el ciberfeminismo, me apremia la gobernanza de internet y el cómo se construye y disputa la raza, la identidad y la visibilidad, en general y en lo particular del contexto latinoamericano. Indago en la tecnología educativa y lo que se vuelve legible o invisible cuando el cuidado y el aprendizaje se diseñan dentro de un sistema encapsulado. Compro libros por vocación.

current interests

In short: I'm interested in how what we document, describe and catalogue decides what can later be known and remembered about people, and in building technology in our best interests rather than deepening social divides and natural depletion.

My current work centres on the invisibilisation of care work, particularly feminised care, and on the broader absence of a politics of care on the internet: infrastructure, protocols, databases, platforms and UX/UI. Digital colonial archives are my current site of inquiry, and I notice how this pattern repeats itself across digital systems more generally. I'm drawn to care ethics, hauntology and critical fabulation as ways of mapping the futures that have been left out and the possibilities still waiting to be embraced. I like reading, writing, and building counter-archives, interactive maps and speculative interfaces. I am, and stand, in cyberfeminism; internet governance matters urgently to me, as does how race, identity and visibility are constructed and contested online — in general, and specifically within the Latin American context. I explore educational technology and what becomes legible or invisible when care and learning are designed within an enclosed system. I buy books as a calling.

professional experience

My path runs from startups in digital payments (the early years of fintech in Mexico), to public-sector programmes in technology investment and sustainable development, to global tech.

At Microsoft LATAM I spent five years governing complex technology agreements with governments, universities and regulated financial institutions in Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay and Chile, working alongside legal, compliance and finance teams. There I also co-founded the Indigenous pillar of Microsoft Mexico's employee resource groups, through which I organised a joint event with the INPI Museum, and served as Microsoft Mexico's Sustainability Champion.

Now at the intersection of feminist technology, politics of care and cultural memory.
